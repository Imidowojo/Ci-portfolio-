The Artist

Story & Process

Inspired by human connections and the moments shared, Caleb draws influence from the reality and spontaneity of life. He works with any available medium to create and expand his knowledge, often beginning with adumbration and allowing the painting to evolve through layers of colours and form.

His paintings serve as a point of contact and reflection for the viewer, illustrating his artistic journey and evolution in storytelling. As he continues to experiment with new techniques and concepts, his work reflects emotions and his ongoing journey.
